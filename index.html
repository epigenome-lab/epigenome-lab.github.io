KRIBB · Aging Convergence Research Center

Decoding the
Epigenome of
Aging & Disease

We investigate how epigenetic modifications—especially DNA methylation— govern cellular memory, disease pathogenesis, and the biology of aging. Our multi-omics approach bridges computational analysis and molecular biology to discover novel biomarkers and therapeutic targets.
