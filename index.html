KRIBB · Aging Convergence Research Center

Decoding the
Epigenome of
Aging & Disease

We investigate how epigenetic modifications—especially DNA methylation— govern cellular memory, disease pathogenesis, and the biology of aging. Our multi-omics approach bridges computational analysis and molecular biology to discover novel biomarkers and therapeutic targets.

What We Do

Research Areas

View All Research
🧬

DNA Methylation

We identify disease biomarkers and therapeutic targets through analysis of DNA methylation patterns.

🫀

MASLD / MASH

We elucidate the epigenomic mechanisms of metabolic dysfunction-associated liver disease and develop innovative therapeutic strategies.

⏳

Aging Epigenomics

We develop SMARTor epigenome editing technology targeting aging-associated epigenomic alterations.

🔬

Multi-omics

We perform integrative multi-omics analyses including RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, ChIP-seq, and ATAC-seq.
